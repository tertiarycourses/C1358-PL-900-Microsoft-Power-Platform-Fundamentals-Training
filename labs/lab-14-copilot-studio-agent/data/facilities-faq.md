# Contoso Facilities — Frequently Asked Questions

This document is the knowledge source for the Contoso Facilities Assistant agent.

## Opening hours

The Contoso facilities helpdesk is staffed Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 6:00pm.
Urgent requests raised outside these hours are picked up by the on-call duty officer,
who can be reached at extension 4400. Buildings are accessible to badge holders from
6:00am to 10:00pm on weekdays and 8:00am to 6:00pm on Saturdays. The North campus is
closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## How long will my request take?

Target response times depend on the priority assigned to the request:

| Priority | First response | Target resolution |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Urgent   | 30 minutes     | Same working day  |
| High     | 2 hours        | 1 working day     |
| Medium   | 1 working day  | 3 working days    |
| Low      | 2 working days | 10 working days   |

Urgent is reserved for anything affecting safety, security or the ability of a team to
work at all — for example a power failure, a water leak or a failed door access system.

## What counts as each category?

- **Maintenance** — repairs to the building or its services: plumbing, heating, air
  conditioning, lighting, doors and access control.
- **Equipment** — printers, monitors, chairs, desks, projectors and other movable assets.
- **Supplies** — consumables such as paper, toner, stationery and first aid stock.
- **Room Setup** — furniture layout, audio-visual setup and catering access for meetings.
- **Other** — anything that does not fit the categories above.

## Do I need approval?

Any request with an estimated cost above SGD 500 is routed automatically to the
facilities manager for approval before work begins. You do not need to seek approval
yourself — the workflow does it. Requests below SGD 500 are actioned directly by the
facilities coordinator.

## How do I book a conference room?

Rooms 301 A on the North campus and 401 B on the East campus are bookable conference
rooms with a capacity of 14 and 24 respectively. Room 233 on the South campus seats six
and is available for informal meetings without a booking. Raise a Room Setup request if
you need a specific furniture layout or audio-visual equipment prepared in advance.

## Who do I contact about a security or safety issue?

Do not raise a facilities request for anything that presents an immediate danger.
Call building security on extension 4000 straight away, then log a request afterwards so
the follow-up work is tracked.

## Can I track my request?

Yes. Every request is stored with a status of New, In Progress, Completed or Cancelled.
You can check the status in the Facility Request App, or ask this assistant.

## What if my request was cancelled?

A request is cancelled when it duplicates an existing one, when the work falls to another
team such as IT, or when the manager declines the spend at approval. The facilities
coordinator records the reason on the request record.
